BUDA-CHAT

Moment of Silence

One pause per day. Five minutes. No apps to configure, no data collected — the pause lives here, in this page, and ends with a bell.

5:00

Do nothing. The bell will end the silence.

The silence is over. Carry it with you. Return tomorrow — this pause is designed for once a day.
Part of the BUDA-CHAT open specification — pausability: every persuasive system should ship with a non-bypassable daily pause. This page collects nothing: no cookies, no analytics, no accounts. Spec & design argument: Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24084547. Text & code: CC BY-SA 4.0.
